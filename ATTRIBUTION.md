## Important note for public distribution

Components marked **"Not stated"** ship without an explicit license. Absence of
a license generally means "all rights reserved" by default. Before relying on
this repository as a public redistribution, verify each source's terms, or
remove/replace the unlicensed components. The MIT- and Apache-licensed
components are safe to redistribute with attribution preserved.

Original `LICENSE` files have been kept inside their component folders wherever
they existed.
